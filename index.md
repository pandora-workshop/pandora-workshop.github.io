# About the Workshop

The 1st PANDORA Workshop  will  take place at [EMNLP 2026](https://2026.emnlp.org/) in Budapest, Hungary!

Modern NLP systems are built on a simplifying assumption: that there exists a single correct answer. Yet in many domains, what is harmful, fair, polite, or persuasive—there is no single truth. Human disagreement is not noise to be removed, but a reflection of pluralism: diverse perspectives shaped by culture, context, and lived experience.

As NLP increasingly relies on human feedback to align models, the field faces a fundamental choice. Do we continue to collapse diversity into a single consensus, or do we design systems that can represent and reason over multiple, sometimes conflicting, viewpoints? This is not only a technical question, but a societal one: about whose values are encoded in AI systems and how they shape the world at scale.
The workshop PANDORA (Pluralistic AI & NLP: Diversity-aware, Sociotechnical, Responsible Alignment) is a call to rethink this paradigm. We envision NLP systems that are diversity-aware, context-sensitive, and socially grounded. PANDORA aims to advance a new generation of methods, metrics, and practices for pluralistic AI across topics of : 

- **Data:** Eliciting and representing pluralistic judgments, and separating error from meaningful variation.
- **Modeling & Alignment:** Learning from diverse and conflicting feedback without collapsing to one single "best" view.
- **Evaluation:** Context-aware assessment beyond single-ground-truth metrics.
- **Normative & Sociotechnical Questions:** Determining when pluralism is beneficial, when consensus is required, and understanding the resulting trade-offs.
